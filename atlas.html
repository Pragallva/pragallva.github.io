Heatwave Atlas

An interactive atlas of blocking-driven summer heatwaves in 18 Northern Hemisphere regions, from the MPI Grand Ensemble (MPI-ESM1.2-LR, 5 members, historical 1850–2014 and SSP3-7.0 2015–2100, June–August). Pick a region on the map to see how often heatwaves occur, what drives them at the surface, and how they are projected to change in a warmer world.
