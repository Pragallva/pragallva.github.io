Methods and definitions

The atlas shows blocking-driven summer heatwaves in 18 regions of the Northern Hemisphere. They come from the MPI Grand Ensemble (MPI-ESM1.2-LR), members 1–5, historical (1850–2014) and SSP3-7.0 (2015–2100), June–August.

Using the atlas

Click a box on the map, or its code in the list, to see its figure. The Heatwaves view shows four panels: heatwave days per decade, the fraction of heatwave days in each surface-flux regime, heatwave surface temperature by regime, and the local wave activity of the heatwaves. The LWA distribution view shows the probability density of daily summer LWA at the box's grid points. The sidebar gives the number of heatwaves and heatwave days in the box, and the heatwave days per decade in each period, and two bars with the share of heatwave days in each surface-flux regime: historical (1850s–2010s) and SSP3-7.0 (2020s–2090s).

Regions

Each continent is split into a West and an East half and three latitude bands: southern (S), central (C) and northern (N). The boxes are clipped to land using Natural Earth 1:110m country outlines. A heatwave belongs to a box when its centroid falls inside it.

Heatwave days

A heatwave's days are its persistence: the number of days with T > TP90 at a grid point in the heatwave core, within ±10 days around the block peak. Heatwave days in a box are the sum over its heatwaves, all 5 members together. The sidebar's days per decade are averages over the 1850s–2010s (historical) and the 2020s–2090s (SSP3-7.0).

Panels of the Heatwaves figure

  • (a) Heatwave days per decade, the total over all 5 members. The trend lines are one joined (continuous piecewise-linear) least-squares fit to the decadal values. The historical and SSP3-7.0 segments meet at the 2010s, the transition decade (2015). The dashed line marks 2015, the start of SSP3-7.0.
  • (b) Fraction of heatwave days by regime, 5 members pooled. The regimes are energy-limited, transitional, moisture-limited and atmosphere-to-surface heating.
  • (c) Heatwave surface temperature by regime. This is the radiative skin temperature Ts = [(rlus − (1 − ε) rlds) / (εσ)]1/4, with ε = 0.996, from daily upward and downward longwave flux. It is averaged over a ±10° box around the heatwave centroid and days −3 to +3. Each line is the mean of a regime's heatwaves within ±10 years (at least 1 event), with shading of ±1 standard error.
  • (d) Local wave activity (LWA), averaged over 5–10 km height, the LWA composite box around the event and days −3 to +3. The line is the mean of all heatwaves in the box (all regimes) within ±10 years, with shading of ±1 standard error.

Panels (c) and (d) show only these running means, with the y-axis fitted to them in each box.

LWA distribution

This is daily LWA averaged over 5–10 km height at every grid point (1.875°) whose centre lies in the box, June–August, members 1–5. It is area-weighted (cos latitude) and uses 0.5 m s−1 bins. The curves show all years (1850–2100), historical (1850–2014) and SSP3-7.0 (2015–2100). Vertical lines mark 65 m s−1, the blocking threshold, and the box's 88th percentile over all years.
